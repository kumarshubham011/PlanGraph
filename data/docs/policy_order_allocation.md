# Order Allocation Policy When Supply Is Short

Type: policy. Owner: Sales Operations.

When there are not enough units to ship every open order, allocate in this order:

1. Orders with the earliest customer due date.
2. Orders that count toward a territory that is under its quarterly target.
3. Everything else, oldest order first.

Partial shipments are allowed for reseller orders when at least 70% of the order value can ship. Online orders always ship complete.
