# Expedite Policy

Type: policy. Owner: Supply Planning.

Expediting means paying extra to get a purchase order delivered faster, usually with premium freight.

- Expedite only when a late or at-risk purchase order blocks open customer orders. Do not expedite to rebuild safety stock.
- Premium freight typically costs 8% to 12% of the purchase order value and saves 5 to 7 days.
- A planner can approve an expedite cost of up to $5,000. Above $5,000 the Supply Manager must approve.
- Before expediting, check whether stock in another location can cover the need (see the Stock Transfer procedure). A transfer is almost always cheaper.
- Record every expedite decision with the alert it resolves, so Finance can track the cost against the revenue protected.
