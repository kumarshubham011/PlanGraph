# Procedure: Monthly S&OP Cycle

Type: standard operating procedure. Owner: S&OP Lead.

S&OP (sales and operations planning) is the monthly meeting cycle that lines up demand, supply and financial targets.

- Week 1: demand review. Compare last month's orders with the forecast and agree the new forecast.
- Week 2: supply review. Check purchase orders, production capacity and stock against the forecast.
- Week 3: reconciliation. Compare the plan with the quarterly sales targets by territory and list the gaps.
- Week 4: executive S&OP. Decide on the gaps: expedite, re-source, re-allocate stock or accept the miss.

Reseller orders arrive in one large batch at the end of each month, so the supply review focuses on that batch.
