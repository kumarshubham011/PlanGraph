# Procedure: Scrap Review and Quality Hold

Type: standard operating procedure. Owner: Quality.

- Normal scrap is well below 1% of units on a work order.
- If a work centre scraps more than 5% of units over a week, Quality places the work centre on hold and starts a root cause review.
- Common causes by work centre: Paint and Specialized Paint (paint process failed, primer process failed, colour incorrect), Frame Welding (stress test failed), Frame Forming (drill size or drill pattern incorrect).
- While a work centre is on hold, Production Planning re-plans the affected work orders and tells Supply Planning which customer orders may slip.
- Scrapped units must be re-built, which uses extra components. Check component stock after any scrap spike.
