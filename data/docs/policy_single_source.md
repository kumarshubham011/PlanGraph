# Single-Source Parts Policy

Type: policy. Owner: Purchasing.

A single-source part has exactly one approved vendor. If that vendor is late, there is no alternate vendor to switch to.

- Single-source parts must carry at least 4 weeks of safety stock.
- Metal sheet stock for frames (Metal Sheet 2 to Metal Sheet 7) is single-sourced from Custom Frames, Inc.
- Lock Ring is single-sourced from Bergeron Off-Roads.
- For a late single-source purchase order the only options are: expedite the order, accept a partial shipment, or re-sequence production to build the products that do not need the part.
- Purchasing reviews the single-source list every quarter and tries to qualify a second vendor for the highest-volume parts.
