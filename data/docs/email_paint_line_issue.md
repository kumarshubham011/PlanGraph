# Email: Paint work centre quality problem

Type: internal email. From: Production Supervisor. To: Quality, Production Planning.

Subject: High scrap at Paint

Over the last three weeks the Paint work centre has been scrapping between a quarter and 40% of frames with the reason "Paint process failed". The humidity controller in the main paint booth is faulty and the finish is blistering.

Maintenance has ordered a replacement controller and expects to fit it within a week. Until then we are painting at reduced volume. Frames that pass through Paint may be short for final assembly, so some bike orders could slip.
