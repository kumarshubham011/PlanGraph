# Planning glossary

Type: reference. Owner: S&OP Lead.

- BOM (bill of materials): the list of components needed to build a product, level by level.
- PO (purchase order): an order we send to a vendor for parts.
- Work order: an instruction to the plant to build a quantity of a product.
- Scrap: units that fail quality checks during production and cannot be sold.
- Lead time: days between placing a purchase order and receiving the parts.
- Weeks of cover: stock on hand divided by weekly demand.
- Stockout: running out of a part or product.
- Revenue at risk: the value of open customer orders that cannot ship because of a supply problem.
- Quota period: the roughly quarterly period a sales target applies to.
