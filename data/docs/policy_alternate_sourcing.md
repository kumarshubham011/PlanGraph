# Alternate Sourcing Policy

Type: policy. Owner: Purchasing.

When the primary vendor for a part cannot deliver on time, a planner may re-source the open quantity to an alternate vendor.

- The alternate vendor must already be approved to supply the part and must be active.
- Prefer vendors with a credit rating of 1 (superior) or 2 (excellent). Vendors rated 4 or 5 need Purchasing Manager approval.
- A price premium of up to 10% over the primary vendor's standard price is allowed without extra approval.
- Compare lead times: an alternate vendor only helps if its lead time is shorter than the expected delay of the original order.
- Do not cancel the original purchase order until the alternate vendor has confirmed the delivery date in writing.
