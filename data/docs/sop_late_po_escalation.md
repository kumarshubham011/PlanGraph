# Procedure: Late Purchase Order Escalation

Type: standard operating procedure. Owner: Purchasing.

- 1 to 2 days late: monitor. Many deliveries arrive within two days of the due date.
- 3 days late: the buyer contacts the vendor and asks for a confirmed ship date in writing.
- 7 days late: escalate to the Supply Manager. Check which open customer orders depend on the parts and decide whether to expedite or re-source.
- 14 days late: the Purchasing Manager calls the vendor's account manager, and the vendor's scorecard is reviewed.

An order counts as late when its due date has passed and nothing has been received.
