# Memo: How sales targets work

Type: finance memo. From: FP&A. To: Sales, S&OP.

- Every sales rep has a sales quota for each quota period. A quota period is about one quarter long.
- A territory's target is the sum of the quotas of the sales reps who cover it.
- Only reseller orders placed through a sales rep count toward quota. Online orders do not.
- Revenue counts when the order ships. An open order that cannot ship before the period ends does not count.
- Historically sales reps reach about 84% of quota on average, so a territory tracking at 85% is normal and a territory below 80% needs attention.
