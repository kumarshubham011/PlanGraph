# Safety Stock Policy

Type: policy. Owner: Supply Planning.

Safety stock is extra inventory held to absorb late deliveries and demand above forecast.

- Purchased components: 2 weeks of forecast demand. Single-source components: 4 weeks.
- Finished bikes are mostly built to order, so finished goods safety stock is kept low.
- A part is flagged when projected stock falls below zero within the 6-week planning horizon.
- Weeks of cover = units on hand divided by forecast weekly demand. Below 2 weeks of cover the planner must act the same day.
