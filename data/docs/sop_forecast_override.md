# Procedure: Forecast Override

Type: standard operating procedure. Owner: Demand Planning.

The statistical forecast can be overridden when the planner knows something the model does not.

- Valid reasons: a confirmed promotion, a new reseller, a product launch or a product being discontinued.
- When actual demand is more than 1.5 times the forecast for a product group, the demand planner must review it within two working days and either raise the forecast or mark the spike as a one-off.
- Every override needs a written reason and an end date.
